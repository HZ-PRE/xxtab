The installer extracts the signed wireguard.exe runtime from the original MSI,
renames that private copy to wd.exe, and places it under xxtab\runtime. It is used
only for xxtab-managed tunnel services. The installer neither installs nor
modifies the separate WireGuard desktop application. Existing installations
are preserved.
